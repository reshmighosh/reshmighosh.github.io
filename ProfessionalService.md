# Professional Service

This page collects conference, journal, and community service activities across reviewing, panel participation, and related academic service.

## Organizing, Program Committee, and Community Leadership

- **ICLR 2025 Workshop on Bidirectional Human-AI Alignment**: Organizer and Program Committee Member for the [ICLR workshop page](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/workshop/23986) and [project page](https://bialign-workshop.github.io/2025).
- **CHI 2025 SIG on Bidirectional Human-AI Alignment: Emerging Challenges and Opportunities**: Organizer for the [CHI program page](https://programs.sigchi.org/chi/2025/program/content/189752), the related [Berkeley publication page](https://www.ischool.berkeley.edu/research/publications/2025/bidirectional-human-ai-alignment-emerging-challenges-and-opportunities), and the [ACM entry](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706599.3716291).
- Across the ICLR 2025 workshop and CHI 2025 SIG, helped coordinate 200+ paper submissions, invited esteemed speakers, and collaboration across reviewers, authors, cross-functional committee members, and area chairs.
- **CIKM 2024 Tutorial on Agentic Systems**: Organized and delivered the tutorial [Frontiers of Large Language Model-Based Agentic Systems - Construction, Efficacy and Safety](https://frontiers-of-ai-agents-tutorial.github.io/), with the [ACM CIKM 2024 tutorial entry here](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3627673.3679105).
